# 📱 Motorola Sales Dashboard

A comprehensive **Power BI Sales Dashboard** built to analyze Motorola's sales performance across different cities, brands, mobile models, payment methods, and time periods. This dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor KPIs, identify sales trends, and make data-driven decisions.

> This project demonstrates Power BI's capabilities in interactive reporting, business intelligence, and sales analytics.

---

# 📖 Project Overview

The Motorola Sales Dashboard provides a centralized view of sales performance by combining multiple business metrics into a single interactive dashboard. It enables users to monitor sales trends, evaluate product performance, analyze customer behavior, and identify regional sales opportunities.

The dashboard is fully interactive with slicers that allow users to filter data by month, brand, mobile model, payment method, and day.

# 📊 Business Insights

The dashboard provides valuable insights, including:

- Strong overall sales performance with **₹769M** in total revenue.
- Consistent monthly sales indicate stable market demand.
- UPI and Debit Card are the most preferred payment methods.
- Certain cities contribute significantly more revenue than others.
- Top-performing mobile models generate a substantial share of total sales.
- Customer ratings suggest a generally positive buying experience.
- Brand comparison helps identify market leaders and growth opportunities.
- Sales vary across weekdays, indicating customer purchasing patterns.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard insights:
